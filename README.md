# Elastic-Stability-and-buckling-diagram-generator
Interactive computational tool to visualize and analyze structural stability phenomena in thin structures , columns and beams. The system tries to generate diagrams that illustrate the relationship between load , deformation , stress state , and stability behavior . Enables user to explore elastic buckling , plastic buckling and post buckling resp
The tool is intended for engineering education , structural analysis and research allowing users to dynamically construct diagrams used in elastic stability theory , column buckling analysis and trefftz type graphical stability methods. 
The system generates diagram that help users understand :
-Critical load conditions 
-Stable and unstable equilibrium states
- Elastic and plastic buckling behavior
- Post buckling deformation paths
- Energy and potential stability landscapes
